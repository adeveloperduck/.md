# Adding Background Music

Your website now has background music functionality! Here's what you need to do:

## Get the Audio File

1. **Download the audio from the YouTube video**:
   - Go to: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obcrj5tZpNE
   - Use a YouTube to MP3 converter like:
     - https://ytmp3.cc/
     - https://y2mate.com/
     - https://www.onlinevideoconverter.com/

2. **Rename and place the file**:
   - Save the audio file as `calm-sunday.mp3`
   - Put it in the root directory of your website (same folder as index.html)

## What's Already Added

- ✅ Mute button in top-left corner
- ✅ Background music player (loops automatically)
- ✅ Low volume setting (15%) for ambient music
- ✅ Auto-starts after loading screen completes
- ✅ Handles browser auto-play restrictions

## How It Works

- Music starts automatically after the loading screen
- Click the mute button to pause/resume
- Music loops continuously while browsing the site
- Respects browser auto-play policies

Once you add the `calm-sunday.mp3` file, the music will work automatically!
